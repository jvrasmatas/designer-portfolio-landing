What I do

Services

01 Album Cover Design

From single artwork to full album packaging — I design covers that visually translate the sound and emotion of a release, making an immediate impact on streaming platforms and physical formats.

02 Artist Branding

Building a consistent visual universe for an artist — logo, color palette, typography, and graphic language that communicate who they are across every platform and touchpoint.

03 Art Direction

Creative direction for photo shoots, music videos and campaign rollouts — defining the visual concept, mood, references and aesthetic that aligns with the project's artistic vision.

04 Promotional Material

Social media assets, press kits, posters, flyers and digital content designed to support launches and tours — maintaining visual consistency across every format.

05 Visual Identity

Complete visual identity systems for labels, studios and music-related brands — from concept to delivery, covering every graphic element needed to establish a strong presence.

06 Motion & Video

Animated visuals, lyric videos, visualizers and motion graphics that bring still designs to life — crafted for social media, streaming platforms and live show screens.
